Our Admits 2018


We at Imperial Overseas Education are a highly energetic organization of young individuals who are working as a team in the education sector. For the last 7 years, we have counseled students for higher education for various countries like USA, UK, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Dubai and Canada.

From a humble beginning in 2009, our team has transformed Imperial to a trusted organization with 7 branches and over 35 team members (2 PHD, 10 Masters from abroad) in India, UAE & Singapore. The diversity of our student pool covers the entire academic spectrum, from STEM to Humanities. In addition to our size we have received accreditation from AIRC, thereby confirming us to have high ethical standards in Counseling. Further, Higher Education Review Magazine has also recognized us as "Overseas Education Consultancy of the Year 2017". (https://www.thehighereducationreview.com/magazines/overseas-consultancy-special-november-2017/). We also have relations with over 50 Schools in USA.
