Mr. Banit Singh Sawhney

Chief Executive Officer

Banit is a dreamer ready to experiment at a whim and therefore also adventurous nature. His idea of a happy time is food, drinks and friends.

He is the one holding the team together and started with a Bachelors in Mechanical Engineering from K.J. Somaiya College in 2005 and MBA from Symbiosis Institute of Business Management in Marketing in 2007.

He has worked in MNC's in a Business Development role for over 6 years before moving to build his Entrepreneurial stint of Imperial Group to promote sound education among the future citizens.

He takes care of overall functioning of the organization and is well connected to an exhaustive network of all verticals including Industry stalwarts, educational establishments and peers alike.
