Mr. Rahul Bhat

Sr. Counselor

Rahul is an Engineer with over 8 years of industry experience in the field of Mechanical Engineering and has been associated with MNCs like Larsen & Toubro Ltd and Whirlpool of India Ltd as well as startups in the energy sector like Urjas Energy Solutions Pvt Ltd and Grassroots Energy Inc. He has completed his M.Tech in Energy Systems Engineering from Indian Institute of Technology Bombay in 2012, and Bachelors in Mechanical Engineering from Sardar Patel College of Engineering, Mumbai University in 2007.

His academic background makes it easy for him to guide and counsel students for higher studies overseas. Given his passion for Mathematics, Rahul also trains students for GRE, SAT, GMAT. He loves to play chess, badminton, and football and goes on regular treks and runs marathons as well.
