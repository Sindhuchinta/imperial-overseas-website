Mr. Nilanjan Dasgupta

Sr. Counsellor & Trainer

Nilanjan has over 12 years of experience in the academic industry with roles ranging from a Junior Research Fellow, Research Scholar, Lecturer and Department Coordinator, Trainer and Counselor.

He has pursued his Bachelors of Science in Physics from Scottish Church College, Calcutta and Masters of Science in Physics from Department of Physics, University of Pune. Nilanjan has been a Government Fellowship holder, attended and published research papers in International Journals and National Conferences. He is also a 'Lifetime Member' of the prestigious Indian Physics Association. Due to his vast exposure to research related areas and academic background, his expert advice helps students in making the right career choice.
