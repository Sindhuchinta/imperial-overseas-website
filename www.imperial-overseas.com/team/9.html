Ms. Nisha Rao

Sr. Counsellor

You would not realize how outdoorsy Nisha is, at first glance, but she is an active participant in various outdoor adventurous activities such as Hikking, Trekking and sporting activities like Throwball, Swimming, Cycling, etc.

She has BA in Psychology and Sociology from Sophia College and also pursued a Diploma in HRM from Welingkar Institute of Management. In addition to this, Nisha also holds a PGD in HRM from K.C College of Management. She has been counselling students for higher studies for over 10 years now with extensive knowledge of education scenario in countries like Australia, Singapore, Canada, Germany, USA and New Zealand.
