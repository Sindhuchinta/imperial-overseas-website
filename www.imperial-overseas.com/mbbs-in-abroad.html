PHILIPPINES
PHILIPPINES
  • English speaking country with English as the medium of instruction.
  • Highest passing percentage in MIC screening test in the first attempt.
  • Excellent hand on practice and clinical exposure in hospitals.
  • Tropical climatic conditions like india with similar disease pattern.
  • American Pattern of education, Called as MD - Doctor of Medicine.
  • Indian Food / canteens available on campus.
RUSSIA
RUSSIA
  • Out of the top 100 medical colleges in the world , about 30 in Russia.
  • Well established medical education system for almost 20 years.
  • Advanced teaching options with excellent equipment and facilities.
  • Russian language is taught as a part of the curriculum.
  • Specializations for students graduating from russia for their further education in Russia
CHINA
CHINA
  • More than *85% of China returned students already registered with MCI.
  • Easy Alignment with indian education system.
  • Internship/residency in government hospitals.
  • English is the medium of Instruction, making it convenient for Indian students.
CARIBBEAN (Clinical Rotation USA)
CARIBBEAN (Clinical Rotation USA)
  • Caribbean Schools offer high quality education based on US pattern of education.
  • Easy absorbabiility for students looking to complete their MD studies in USA (Resident Program in USA).
  • No MCI screening for practicing in India**
  • Taught by leading specialists from USA.
  • High US MLE passing rate.
EUROPE
EUROPE
  • MBBS in Europe is a 6 years program.
  • Well established & Economically viable MBBS Degree.
  • Easy access to countries in Europe.
  • Indian Food / canteens available on campus.
  • High post study absorption chances in Eurrope.
