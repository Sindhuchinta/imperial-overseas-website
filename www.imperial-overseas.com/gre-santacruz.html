Equip Yourself to Take up GRE test 2017

The Graduate Record Examination, which is abbreviated as GRE is a computer based test that you have to take up if you are aspiring to do higher studies abroad. While there are a few Universities that are not particular about GRE scores, a good GRE score is sure to help you gain entry as an influencing factor into the most prestigious and top rated universities

What type of test is it?

The GRE is a test that is not based on any specific field of study. It is basically a test to measure thinking, reasoning and analytical skills that you have been accumulating all these years. However the GRE test is available in 2 formats

  • GRE General Test
  • GRE Subject Test
About GRE General Test

The GRE General test is the most common test that anyone aspiring to study abroad has to take up and it is accepted in Singapore, Canada, USA and many other countries. The GRE General Test measures the candidate's

  • Critical thinking
  • Writing skills
  • Analytical skills
  • Verbal and quantitative reasoning ability
GRE training- Is it necessary?

The GRE general tests are actually divided into 3 sections which include

  • Verbal reasoning
  • Quantitative reasoning and
  • Analytical writing
