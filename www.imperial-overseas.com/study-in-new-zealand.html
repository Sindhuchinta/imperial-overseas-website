New Zealand

The fascinating Island country of New Zealand has been known for its beautiful landscapes and peaceful views. The predominant official language is English, owing to its English colonization and majority of the population is of European descent. It has also been known to have high immigration rates making it an indigenous community. It is identified as the world’s most stable and well governed states. Other than education, New Zealand is also known for its tourism that it attracts every year. It is well known for its movie trails owing to movies shot in the countryside.

EDUCATION SCENARIO

New Zealand has 8 state funded universities, 16 institutes of Technology and Polytechnics (ITP) and about 550 Private Training Establishments (PTE) which include English language training as well. Universities offer higher degree-level education. Programs are research-led therefore the PhD education is at the same cost for international students as for local students. In the 2015/16 QS World University Rankings, all eight of New Zealand’s universities were in the top 100 in at least one subject. ITPs and a few larger PTEs offer vocational degree-level education. PTEs tend to offer specific vocational programs, mainly at certificate and diploma level.

EXPENSES

Education fees in the country of New Zealand are dependent on the location of the school as well as the field of course that the students wish to take. On an average, international students may want to consider the costs of

Diplomas/Certificates NZ$ 10000 to NZ$ 15000 per year
Undergraduate NZ$ 18000 to NZ$ 32000 per year
Postgraduate NZ$ 20000 to NZ$ 37000 per year
PhD NZ$ 6500 to NZ$ 9000

Living expenses would differ from person to person, but on an average one can calculate a total of NZ$ 15000 – NZ$ 20000.
